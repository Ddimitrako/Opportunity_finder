# Product

Opportunity Finder helps a new, solo-first software company identify and win Greek/EU public procurements and specific private-sector software opportunities.

## Why this exists

Procurement feeds contain planning records, named invitations, awards, incumbent extensions, hardware purchases, and genuine open software work. Relevance tags alone do not answer whether an opportunity is accessible, winnable, deliverable, and worth the bid effort.

The product turns fragmented evidence into a short, explainable action queue optimized for the first commercial win.

## Who it is for

- A senior full-stack founder operating solo-first with selective partners.
- Work based on open-source deployment/customization, custom dashboards/web apps, workflows, APIs, integrations, and data/BI.
- Initial quick-win budget of €5k–€30k, core work to €80k, and larger stretch/partner opportunities.

## What it is not

- A generic market-news dashboard or full CRM.
- A guarantee that a tender is legally accessible or commercially winnable.
- A license/hardware resale catalog.
- An opaque AI ranking system without evidence and deterministic safeguards.

## Durable product rules

Lifecycle correctness comes before semantic fit. Every recommendation must expose its reasons, risks, unknowns, and next action. AI enriches evidence and ambiguity; it does not override hard procurement gates.
